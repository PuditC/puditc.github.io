Before you write

Four lines is enoughSpeeds up the reply
  1. What it is. One or two sentences on the product and who uses it.
  2. Where it stands. Idea, existing codebase, or a running system that needs work.
  3. What you need from me. Build it, extend it, review it, or unstick something specific.
  4. Timeline and budget range. Rough is fine — it decides whether I can take it on at all.

What happens next

I reply within a day or two, usually with questions rather than a quote. If the scope is clear I send a fixed price and a delivery date; if it isn't, I suggest a short paid discovery instead of guessing.
