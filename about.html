Senior developerBangkokSix years, three companiesFreelance & private projects only

I started on the infrastructure side — Windows Server, Active Directory and Exchange for banks, exchanges and insurers. Fixing other people's systems at 2am is a good education in what to build.

Since 2021 I've been writing them instead: a group-insurance core rewrite, a customer claims app in Flutter, and now web and application modules on Azure as a senior developer at PTT Digital Solutions. The work is usually less about new features than about making the second year of a system survivable.

On the side I ship small things end to end — a Bitcoin block clock, a gold-portfolio tracker, a running-form analyser, a multiplayer deduction game — mostly to keep the deploy-to-production muscle working.
